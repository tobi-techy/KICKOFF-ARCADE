## Missing Improvements & Fun Enhancements

### 🎮 Gameplay Mechanics

High Impact:
1. Running animations - You have partial animations but they're not being used. Animate sprites when moving
2. Player switching - Currently stuck with one player; add tap-to-switch or auto-switch to nearest player
3. Set pieces - Corners, free kicks, throw-ins are detected but not implemented as playable moments
4. Ball shadow - Adds depth perception, makes the game feel more polished
5. Goalkeeper diving - GK just moves; add diving saves for shots

Medium Impact:
6. Skill moves - Quick direction changes, feints (double-tap to nutmeg)
7. Lob/chip passes - Currently only ground passes; add aerial balls
8. Headers - When ball is in air near player
9. Offside rule (optional toggle) - Adds tactical depth
10. Fatigue visual - Show tired players (slower animation, sweat particles)

### 📊 Game Feel & Juice

11. Screen shake on goals
12. Goal celebration - Brief animation/zoom on scorer
13. Crowd noise - Ambient cheering that reacts to possession/shots
14. Ball trail - Subtle trail on powerful shots
15. Hit pause - 2-3 frame freeze on powerful kicks (game feel)
16. Commentary text - "Great save!", "Close!", "What a goal!"
17. Replay system - Show goal replay from different angle

### 🏆 Progression & Engagement

18. Match stats screen - Shots, possession %, passes completed
19. Player form/morale - Performance affects next match stats
20. Training mini-games - Penalty shootout, free kick practice
21. Tournament mode - Cup bracket with progression
22. Daily challenges - "Score 3 goals", "Win without conceding"
23. Achievement system - Unlock badges for milestones

### 🎨 Visual Polish

24. Team kit colors on sprites - Use CSS filters or shader to tint sprites to team colors (not just hue-rotate for AI)
25. Field markings animation - Subtle grass sway
26. Weather effects - Rain, snow (affects ball physics)
27. Day/night modes - Different lighting
28. Scoreboard animations - Flip animation on score change

### 🔧 Technical/UX

29. Difficulty levels - AI aggression, reaction time, passing accuracy
30. Control customization - Button layout options
31. Haptic feedback - Vibration on mobile for kicks/goals
32. Pause menu improvements - Formation change mid-game
33. Quick restart - Instant rematch option
